BALLANCE 3D STEREO - HOW TO PLAY
===============================

This folder is the game source. It contains NO Ballance levels,
textures, sounds, installer, or ISO. You add those yourself from
your own copy. Everything the wizard downloads or builds stays
inside this folder. Nothing is installed on your system.

YOU NEED
--------
- Windows 10/11.
- Your own legally bought Ballance (installed folder or ISO).
  Example: C:\Games\Ballance. Install it first if you only have an ISO.
- Internet for the one-time download. Nothing else to install.

PLAY IN 3 STEPS
---------------
1. Copy this folder somewhere writable, for example:
   C:\Games\Ballance-3D-source

2. Double-click Setup-Ballance.bat.
   - Press Y to accept the open-source licenses.
   - When asked, type your Ballance folder and press Enter.
   - Wait. It downloads its own Node/Python/FFmpeg, converts
     your levels, and opens the game. Say N to the optional
     exact-physics question unless you want a 10-20 minute build.

3. Play at http://127.0.0.1:5173/ in your browser.
   Next time, just double-click Launch-Ballance.bat.

CONTROLS
--------
- Arrow keys: roll. Shift + left/right: turn camera.
  Space: raise camera. Escape: pause.
- Gamepad works. Change buttons in Options -> Controls.
- 3D glasses TV? See Options -> 3D Stereo.
